Poetry Toolbox 🧰

Use this quick guide while you work.

LineOne row of words in a poem.
StanzaA group of lines, similar to a paragraph.
RhymeWords with similar ending sounds.
RepetitionRepeating words or sounds for emphasis.
AlliterationRepeating beginning consonant sounds.
OnomatopoeiaA word that imitates a sound.
SimileA comparison using like or as.
MetaphorA comparison saying one thing is another.
PersonificationHuman qualities given to nonhuman things.
ToneThe speaker's or author's attitude.
MoodThe feeling the reader gets.
ThemeA message or lesson developed through the text.
FAST Reader Rule: FIND IT → THINK ABOUT IT → CONNECT IT.
DEVICE → EFFECT → MEANING

Read the Poem 🌧️

Read once for what happens. Read again for pictures, sounds, figurative language, mood changes, and stanza meaning.

After the Rain

The storm had stomped across the town,And rattled every door.It tossed the leaves like tiny boatsAnd made the thunder roar.
But soon the angry clouds moved on,And sunlight touched the street.The raindrops sparkled on the grassLike diamonds at my feet.
A little bird began to sing.The trees stood tall and bright.The world that seemed so dark beforeWas waking into light.
I stepped outside and breathed it in,The cool and shining air.Sometimes a storm must pass us byTo show what still is there.
